## The `herock` head term

Herock Workwear holds the global results for `herock`. Herock Nigeria should aim to:

1. own every result for `herock nigeria` and its variants;
2. appear on page one for `herock` when searched from Nigeria, helped by the domain, the Business Profile and local links;
3. get a brand panel (knowledge panel) for Herock Nigeria Ltd, which needs consistent details across the site, Business Profile, LinkedIn and directories.

Outranking the workwear brand worldwide is not a realistic target, and no amount of on-site work changes that.
